# MSA + SOW Pricing Structure

## Contract Architecture
Use two layers:
- Master Services Agreement (MSA): legal terms, liability, confidentiality, payment framework.
- Statement of Work (SOW): scope, deliverables, timeline, fees, success criteria.

## MSA Clauses (Required)
1. Services and independent contractor status
2. Confidentiality and data handling
3. Client representations and record accuracy
4. Fees, invoicing, and payment terms
5. Contingency fee definition and calculation method
6. Term and termination
7. Limitation of liability and indemnification
8. Compliance with law and non-reliance language
9. Dispute resolution and governing law
10. IP ownership (client data vs provider tools/templates)

## SOW Template Sections
1. Client and property/site identifiers
2. Problem statement
3. Scope inclusions
4. Scope exclusions
5. Deliverables
6. Timeline and milestones
7. Roles/responsibilities
8. Fees and commercial terms
9. Acceptance criteria
10. Change-order process

## Pricing Table
### A) Asset Shield (Contingency)
- Fee: `30%` of realized refund/credit.
- Minimum admin fee: `$750` only if client cancels after filing package completion.
- Invoicing trigger: client receipt of refund/credit confirmation.

### B) Compliance Shield (Monthly Retainer)
- Essential: `$500/month`
- Operator: `$1,200/month`
- Assurance: `$2,500+/month`
- Billing: monthly in advance, net 15.
- Term: 3-month minimum recommended.

### C) Advisory Projects (Fixed Fee)
- Standard: `$7,500`
- Advanced: `$15,000`
- Portfolio: `$30,000+`
- Billing: `50%` at kickoff, `30%` at midpoint, `20%` at delivery.

## Approval and Signature Block
Include:
- Authorized signer name/title
- Signature/date fields
- Property/site list attachment
- Data authorization addendum reference

## Operational Notes
- Keep contingency language precise and auditable.
- Require written scope signoff before filing support begins.
- Store all SOW versions and change orders in one client dossier.
